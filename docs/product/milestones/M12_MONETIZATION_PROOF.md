# M12 — Monetization Proof

## Purpose

Validate that SmartIQ can become a real business, not just a technically capable product.

## Technical Scope

- Commercial instrumentation for activation, repeat use, and payment
- Paid pilot structure
- Plan and entitlement packaging
- Revenue signal reporting

## Product Impact

- Converts product progress into business proof
- Shows whether hosts will actually pay
- Prevents fake SaaS confidence built on internal assumptions

## Definition of Done

- Real usage and payment signals are measurable
- Paid pilots produce decision-grade evidence
- Monetization strategy is based on observed host behavior
