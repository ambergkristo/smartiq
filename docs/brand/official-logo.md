# CherryPick Official Logo

## Official Brand Name

CherryPick

## Current Official Logo Direction

The current official CherryPick logo direction is:

- a minimal twin-cherry icon
- paired with a clean rounded `CherryPick` wordmark
- styled to feel friendly premium, playful precision, warm, clean, and modern

The mark intentionally avoids neon-racing, cyber, casino-glow, and esports styling.

## Rationale

CherryPick is a name about selection, judgment, and a small moment of delight. The twin-cherry icon gives the product a memorable shape that is simple enough for headers, compact product surfaces, favicon exploration, and future social usage.

Using two cherries keeps the symbol recognizable and friendly. Subtly emphasizing one cherry supports the idea of “pick” without making the logo noisy or overly illustrative.

The rounded wordmark keeps the brand approachable while still feeling product-grade and premium.

## Implementation Notes

- The current official in-product CherryPick logo is implemented as an SVG/component-based frontend mark.
- The reusable implementation lives in the frontend as a shared logo component so it can be used in the app header, home entry, and future compact brand placements.
- The logo is intentionally frontend-native and does not depend on a heavy raster image asset.

## Scope Note

This document approves the current logo direction only. A broader UI theme redesign is intentionally out of scope for this task.
