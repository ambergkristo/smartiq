# PPTX FIXER Interview Script

## Purpose

Use this script to validate whether formatting cleanup in existing PowerPoint decks is a painful, frequent, and valuable problem to solve safely.

## Interview Questions

1. What is your role, and how often do you work with PowerPoint files?
2. What kinds of decks do you usually edit or review?
3. How often do you need to clean up formatting in an existing `.pptx` deck?
4. What formatting issues show up most often in the decks you touch?
5. When formatting drift appears, which parts of the deck are usually affected first?
6. Roughly how much time do you spend on formatting cleanup in a typical week or per deck?
7. What do you do today to fix these issues?
8. Which cleanup tasks feel repetitive or low value?
9. If there were a safe tool that corrected high-confidence formatting inconsistencies without changing slide meaning or editability, would you try it? Why or why not?
10. What trust, privacy, or file-safety concerns would you have before using a tool like this?

## Interviewer Notes

- Ask for one recent example deck if the participant can share a sanitized version.
- Probe for concrete before/after examples instead of general opinions.
- Capture exact wording for trust, privacy, and "safe auto-fix" concerns.
